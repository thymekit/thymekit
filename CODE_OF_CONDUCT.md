# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about code, not about people. Assume the other side is trying to make the project
better, because usually they are.

## What is expected

- Judge a change by what it does, not by who wrote it.
- Disagree in the open, with reasons. "This is wrong because X" is a contribution; "this is wrong"
  is not.
- Accept that the answer may be no. A library stays coherent by refusing things, and a refusal is
  about the change, not about you.
- Assume good faith in a badly worded message. Not everyone writes in their first language.

## What is not acceptable

- Personal attacks, insults, or dismissing someone for who they are rather than what they said.
- Harassment of any kind, in issues, pull requests, or private messages that started here.
- Deliberate derailing, repeated bad-faith arguing, or publishing someone's private information.

## If something goes wrong

Write to **318698958+thymekit@users.noreply.github.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer and
stay private. The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close threads, or block accounts —
and will say plainly that a decision was made, without airing the details of the report.

## Scope

This applies in this repository and anywhere someone is representing the project.
